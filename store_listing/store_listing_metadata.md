## 2. Full Description (English · ~2,800 chars)

```text
StockSafe is a fast, 100% offline stock and inventory manager built specifically for small retail shops, kirana stores, supermarkets, and independent businesses.

Most inventory apps store your current stock as a single editable number. One software glitch or accidental tap, and your product counts vanish. StockSafe is built on an immutable, double-entry movement ledger: every inward shipment, sale, return, or audit adjustment is permanently recorded. Your inventory can never be lost or corrupted.

WHY SHOPKEEPERS CHOOSE STOCKSAFE:

1. 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE (NO CLOUD REQUIRED)
Operate seamlessly in basements, dark storerooms, or areas with zero internet. Your data is stored strictly on your phone in a secure local database. No accounts, no logins, no cloud subscriptions.

2. FAST BARCODE SCANNING & CONTINUOUS MODE
Scan barcodes instantly using your phone camera (ML Kit powered). Use Continuous Batch Scan mode to scan whole cartons without ever taking your eyes off the stock. Includes instant vibration and audio beep feedback.

3. AI PACKAGING LABEL OCR
Scan product labels to automatically extract MRP, expiry dates, batch numbers, and net quantities. Populate new items in seconds without manual typing.

4. BLUETOOTH THERMAL RECEIPT & SLIP PRINTING (ESC/POS)
Generate print-ready 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS slips over Bluetooth:
• Stock Audit Discrepancy Sheets
• Inward Stock Delivery Slips
• Low-Stock Purchase Reorder Slips
Share directly to RawBT or Bluetooth POS printers with one tap.

5. BARCODE LABEL STICKER PRINTING
Create printable PDF sticker sheets with Code 128 barcodes or QR codes for loose, repackaged, or unbranded commodities. Supports standard A4 sticker sheets (24-up, 30-up, 40-up) and direct thermal rolls (50x25mm, 50x30mm).

6. SUPPLIER & VENDOR DIRECTORY
Link suppliers to your products. When an item runs low, tap "Call Supplier" directly from your Low-Stock alert screen or dispatch purchase orders via WhatsApp.

7. BILINGUAL VOICE ASSISTANT (ENGLISH & TAMIL)
Hands-free voice operations in retail terminology. Ask "How much sugar stock?" or "குறைந்த இருப்பு காட்டு" to query stock instantly while packing items.

8. AUTOMATIC VERIFIED BACKUPS & CSV EXPORT
• 7-copy rolling local snapshots prevent accidental loss.
• Full Excel / CSV export with every single column included.
• Cryptographic backup verification ensures your backup is valid before restoring.

9. FAIR ONE-TIME PRICING (NO SNEAKY SUBSCRIPTIONS)
Free for up to 100 products. Unlock unlimited products forever with a single one-time payment of ₹499 ($5.99). We never lock your entered data behind a paywall—viewing, stock movements, audits, and backups remain free forever.

Download StockSafe today and keep your shop's inventory accurate, organized, and 100% safe.
```
